LiveCom

Live transcription for show crews

Every channel on a show is transcribed on its own and shown as one chat-style feed, so nobody misses a call on comms. Speech recognition runs on the computer itself, with nothing sent to the internet.

  • Takes channels from Dante Virtual Soundcard, ASIO and other interfaces at once
  • Groups and tabs, with an unread badge and alert when a hidden group talks
  • Hot words such as standby, cue and go are highlighted and always alert
  • OSC and MIDI automations fire from what is said, for example a QLab GO

Dante Visualizer

Dante network signal flow and monitoring

A whole-network view of a Dante system: every transmitter, switch and receiver in one signal-flow diagram, with live signal moving through it. When something goes wrong it says where, down to the switch port.

  • Signal-flow view of devices, switches and routes
  • Clock view: the PTP leader, followers in lock and jitter over time
  • Switch health over SNMP for Netgear M4250 and Luminex GigaCore
  • “Lost on the way”: compares what each transmitter sends with what each receiver gets
  • A searchable history of every problem through the show

Lathe

CD premastering, editing and DDP

A Mac mastering workstation for assembling albums: place the masters, set track marks and pauses, fill in the disc details and deliver a checked DDP image or files.

  • Source and destination timelines for take assembly and editing
  • Track and index marks, pauses and CD text, with a track list beside the timeline
  • Lanes in mono, stereo or 5.1, with per-clip effects chains
  • Loudness metering, a full Meter Bridge window and pre-delivery checks
  • Editable key commands and batch rendering
Mac appMasteringDDP

Prism Meter

All-in-one metering plugin

Every meter a mix or master needs in one plugin window, built from the published standards. Layouts fit the screen it lives on, from a full console view to a single meter on a small display.

  • Loudness to ITU-R BS.1770-5 and EBU R128: momentary, short-term, integrated, LRA, true peak
  • VU, PPM (EBU, BBC, DIN, Nordic) and K-System meters
  • A dual-needle peak and average gauge with crest factor
  • Spectrum with a scrolling spectrogram, and a vectorscope with correlation and width
  • Display presets for 12″, 14″, ultrawide and 7″ screens

Meter Bridge

Standalone multi-source metering

A standalone Mac app that meters live inputs from Dante Virtual Soundcard, Pro Tools outputs or any Core Audio device. Up to six sources share one spectrum, so you can see which keyboard is playing which frequencies.

  • Spectrum overlay: six coloured traces over a 3D history of their sum
  • Quad and row layouts that put four Prism meters on one display
  • An arched LED meter panel with loudness, for an 8″ bar screen
  • One window per display, each with its own sources and settings
